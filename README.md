One original one-star review written by a house cat about its human.
`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line, 128
characters, no trailing newline. The cat brought its human a mouse, a
gift, and the human screamed; the cat's response is to bring a smaller
one next time, so he can learn.
